# Leave room in a brief for its outcome note

corpus-flow caps a page at about 200 lines. A brief written at the cap has no
room for the outcome note that is added when it moves to `done/`.

Done 2026-11-13 in v0.48.0, as part of [brief 19](../briefs/done/19-a-skills-stocktake-and-mcp-pin-review-from-ecc.md): `corpus-flow`'s conventions say that `corpus/lint.sh` caps only wiki pages and that a `done/` brief can run longer, so a brief leaves room for its outcome note.
